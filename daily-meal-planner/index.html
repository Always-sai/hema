    Kitchen notes

    Small habits that make planning stick

    Practical ideas you can use with any recipe, not just the ones on this page.

    Cook once, eat twice

    Double the rice, grains or roasted vegetables when you cook. Tomorrow's lunch then takes five minutes instead of thirty.

    Shop the list, not the shop

    Walk the aisles in the order your shop is laid out. The checklist groups everything by name, so nothing gets bought twice.

    Keep a flexible shelf

    Stock tinned beans, rice, pasta and a good stock. When a plan changes, those staples turn any vegetable into dinner.

    Questions

    Good to know

    Is the meal library real?

    It is a sample set of twelve everyday meals written for this demo. The planner behaves exactly the same way with your own recipes, so you can swap the data for your household favourites.

    How do servings work?

    Each meal has a base serving count. When you change servings in your plan, every ingredient quantity scales to match, and the checklist updates straight away.

    Can I use this on my phone in the shop?

    Yes. The checklist is built for one-handed tapping, with large targets and a progress bar so you can see how much is left.

    Does it save my plan?

    Your plan is stored in this browser only, so it is waiting when you come back on the same device. Reset plan clears it completely.

    What if I am missing an ingredient?

    Tick what you have and leave the rest. The checklist is a shopping aid, not a rulebook — most of these meals work with a sensible swap.
