      How it works

      Three steps, one calm plan

      No setup and no learning curve. The whole flow takes about a minute.

      1

      Choose your meals

      Browse the library and add the meals you actually want to cook today. Breakfast, lunch and dinner all live in the same place.

      2

      Check your list

      Ingredients from every meal are merged into a single shopping list. If two meals need olive oil, it appears once with both amounts.

      3

      Shop and cook

      Tick items off as you put them in the basket. The progress bar keeps your place, and the plan stays on screen while you cook.

      Questions

      Good to know

      Short answers to the things people ask most often.

      Do I need an account to use this?

      No. The planner runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded and there is nothing to sign up for.

      Will my plan still be here if I close the tab?

      Your plan lives in the current page, so reloading or closing the tab starts a fresh plan. If you want to keep a list, use the “Copy list” button and paste it into your notes.

      How are duplicate ingredients handled?

      Ingredients are matched by name. When two meals need the same thing, it appears once and the amounts are shown together, for example “Olive oil — 1 tsp + 1 tbsp”.

      Can I plan more than one day at a time?

      The planner is built for a single day, but you can add as many meals as you like. Adding a week's worth of meals simply gives you a longer shopping list.

      Does it work on a phone?

      Yes. The layout adapts to phones, tablets and desktops, and every button is sized for touch.
