    How it works

    Three small steps, no sign-up, nothing to install.

    1

    Choose your meals

    Use the filters to find breakfasts, lunches, dinners and snacks that fit how you eat and how much time you have.

    2

    Add them to today

    Tap “Add to plan” on any meal card. Your plan groups everything by meal time so the day stays easy to read.

    3

    Shop from the checklist

    Every ingredient from your chosen meals is collected into one list. Tick items off as you shop or as you cook.

    Questions people ask

    Short answers to the things that come up most often.

    Do I need an account to use the planner?

    No. Everything works right here in the page. Your plan lives in this browser tab only, so nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored on a server.

    What happens if I add two meals with the same ingredient?

    The checklist shows that ingredient once and adds a small “×2” marker, so you know to buy enough for both meals.

    Can I plan more than one day at a time?

    The planner is built around a single day, which keeps the list short and the decisions simple. Clear the plan when you are done and start fresh tomorrow.

    Are the meals suitable for specific diets?

    Each meal is tagged as vegetarian, vegan, high protein, gluten free or quick. These are simple guides based on the listed ingredients — always check labels if you have an allergy.

    Will my plan be saved if I close the page?

    No. Closing or refreshing the tab starts a clean plan. That is intentional: it keeps the tool private and free of accounts.
