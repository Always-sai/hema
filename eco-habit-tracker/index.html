      How it works

      Three steps, no pressure

      Greenstep is deliberately small. There is no target to hit and no score to chase.

      1. 1

        Pick from the library

        Choose the actions that fit your day. Add as many or as few as you like — a single tick still counts.

      2. 2

        Tick them off

        Complete an action and check the box. Your streak grows for every day with at least one completed action.

      3. 3

        Watch the pattern

        The seven-day strip shows where your consistency sits. The impact figures are a gentle nudge, not a scoreboard.

      About the numbers

      Honest about the estimates

      Every figure in this tracker is an illustration. Here is exactly what that means.

      Rough averages

      Each action carries a small estimate based on commonly published averages for that activity, rounded to keep things readable.

      Your results will differ

      Local energy sources, product choices and how you use things all change the real figure. Treat the totals as a nudge, not a measurement.

      The habit matters most

      A ticked box you repeat is worth more than a perfect number you never act on. Consistency is the whole point of this tool.

      Your data stays with you. Greenstep has no accounts and no servers. Everything you tick is stored in this browser only, and clearing your browser data will clear your list.

      Questions

      Good to know

      Do I need an account?

      No. Greenstep has no sign-up and no login. Your list is stored in your browser's local storage on this device.

      How is the streak counted?

      A day counts when you complete at least one action. The streak is the number of consecutive days ending today — or ending yesterday, if you haven't ticked anything yet today.

      Why are the impact numbers marked as estimates?

      Because they are. They are rounded averages for a typical version of each action. Real savings depend on where you live, how your electricity is generated and what you would have done instead.

      Can I add my own actions?

      Yes. Use the form under the action library. Give the action a name and, if you like, a rough CO₂e estimate. It is saved alongside the built-in actions.

      What happens if I clear my browser data?

      Your list, streak and custom actions are removed. There is no cloud backup, so nothing is stored anywhere else.

      Does it work offline?

      Once the page has loaded, yes. Greenstep makes no network requests, so the tracker keeps working without a connection.
