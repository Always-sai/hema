    Good to know

    Short answers to the questions people usually ask before their first session.

    What happens if I close this page?

    Your garden and history are saved in this browser, so they will still be here when you return. A running timer does not keep counting after the page is closed, so simply start a fresh session when you come back.

    Does a paused session still plant anything?

    Only a session that runs all the way to zero plants a sprout. Pausing is always fine and the timer keeps your place, but resetting or skipping a session will not add to your garden.

    Can I change the timer length?

    Yes. Use the session settings to set your focus length, short break, long break, and how many focus sessions come before a long break. Save your changes and the new lengths apply the next time you start a session.

    Is my data sent anywhere?

    No. Your settings, garden, and history stay in your browser's local storage on this device. There is no account, no upload, and no tracking.
