A few things
to know.

Meal prep should make life simpler. Here’s how this calculator fits in.

How are the nutrition targets calculated?

We split your daily calorie goal across breakfast, lunch, and dinner, then estimate protein, carbohydrate, and fat using a balanced ratio. “High protein” shifts the ratio toward protein; “Light & fresh” favors produce and lighter meals. Values are estimates and can vary with ingredients and portions.

Can I use this with allergies or a medical condition?

Use the food preference filters to avoid gluten or dairy, but always check ingredient labels for allergens and cross-contact. This tool is for general meal planning, not medical advice. If you have a medical condition, pregnancy, or specific nutrient needs, work with a qualified clinician or dietitian.

How long will the meals actually keep?

Most cooked meals keep in the refrigerator for 3–4 days when cooled promptly and stored in sealed containers. For a 5- or 7-day plan, freeze portions for later in the week and thaw them safely in the refrigerator. Check food regularly and follow local food-safety guidance.

Does my information leave this page?

No. Your inputs and generated plan are processed in your browser. There are no accounts, analytics, or external services. Copying or printing a plan only happens when you choose to do it.
