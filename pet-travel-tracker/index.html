A few good questions

Before you hit the road.

Simple answers for a little extra confidence.

Where is my pet’s information stored?

Your records stay in this browser using local storage. Pawport does not send your pet’s information to a server. Export a copy for safekeeping, especially before clearing browser data or switching devices.

Will Pawport remind me when it’s time for medication?

The medication schedule shows upcoming doses while Pawport is open. Trip reminders appear in your trip list when a departure is near. This app does not send background notifications, emails, or alerts when the page is closed.

Can I reuse my packing checklist for another trip?

Yes. Saving a new trip creates a fresh copy of your packing items, with every item unchecked. Your original checklist stays as it is, so you can use it again and again.

Is this a replacement for veterinary advice?

No. Pawport is an organizational tool, not a veterinarian. Follow your vet’s instructions for medications, vaccinations, and travel suitability. Contact your vet if you have concerns about your pet’s health.

A simple note about privacy.

Saved locally, not in the cloud. Pet details, health records, medications, packing items, and trips are stored in this browser. They are not sent to a server and do not sync between devices.

Keep a backup. Clearing browser data can remove your information. Use “Export records” to download a JSON copy whenever you need one.

A private travel companion. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to see your saved information. Avoid entering sensitive details on a shared device.
