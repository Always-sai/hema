Good questions

Before you begin.

A few things worth knowing about your renovation plan.

Is this a construction plan?

No. This is a visual planning tool for small DIY projects. It doesn’t account for structural work, plumbing, electrical changes, or local building requirements. For those projects, consult a qualified professional.

How are the costs calculated?

Each material has an illustrative price per square foot or per item. The estimate adds a tool allowance when needed and a 15% contingency. It excludes labor and tax. Use your local supplier’s prices as your final reference.

Will my project be saved?

Yes. Your layout, quantities, room size, and budget are stored locally in this browser when local storage is available. No account is needed. Clearing browser data will remove your saved project.

Can I use this on my phone?

Absolutely. Tap a material’s + button to add it. Drag placed items to move them, or select one and use the quantity controls. Your plan works on phones, tablets, and desktops.
