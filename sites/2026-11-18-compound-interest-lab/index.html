What is Compound Interest? It’s "interest on interest": each year, you earn interest on your starting amount plus all past interest and contributions! Over time, this snowball effect can power up your savings.
How Is It Calculated? Each year: grow current balance by the rate (eg. 5%) and add your annual contribution. Repeat! Try different numbers above to see the difference more years, bigger savings, or higher rates can make.
