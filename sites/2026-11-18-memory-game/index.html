How the Memory Match Game works

  1. Select a difficulty and press Restart to deal fresh cards.
  2. Flip two cards at a time. If they match, they stay face up.
  3. If they do not match, they flip back after a short pause.
  4. Clear all pairs to finish the round and log your score.

Your aim is to match all pairs with the fewest moves and the calmest pace. The timer starts on your first flip and pauses whenever you need a break.

Keyboard & focus-friendly play

Cards are arranged in reading order. You can navigate and flip without a mouse:

Tab / Shift+Tab move focus Space / Enter flip card or press buttons R restart current level P pause or resume timer

If you prefer less animation, your operating system’s “Reduce Motion” setting is respected.
